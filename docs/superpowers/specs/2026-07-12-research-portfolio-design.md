# Research Portfolio Redesign

## Goal

Reframe the Research page from a SmCo-only project report into a project-led research portfolio that represents Yifan Suo's broader computational materials background.

## Information Architecture

1. Broad research introduction covering processing, microstructure, mechanics, and properties.
2. Four research-area indices: Additive Manufacturing; Microstructure Evolution; Multiphysics & Fracture; Data-driven Materials Modeling.
3. SmCo5 as the current flagship project, occupying roughly 40% of the page and linking to the detailed Workflow Console.
4. Four independent academic project records: dual-laser PBF, solid-state battery FE-ANN, MOOSE thermal-shock fracture, and SiC ceramics.
5. A separate Industry R&D section for Shining 3D.
6. A cross-project methods index.

## Content Rules

- Every factual statement must map to the concise CV or verified SmCo brief.
- Dual-laser PBF must visibly retain its `Under review` status and Engineering with Computers venue.
- SmCo numerical evidence remains concise on Research; detailed stage evidence stays in Console.
- No fake scientific imagery, metrics, publication claims, or live project status.
- Missing authentic project images are handled with typography, not placeholders or generated results.

## Visual Direction

Preserve the existing calm scientific-studio language. Use full-width horizontal records with a left index/status column, a central research narrative, and a right methods/contribution column. Avoid a card wall. SmCo receives a larger editorial feature; prior work remains substantial and independently scannable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A first-time visitor can identify at least five distinct academic research projects above the Industry R&D section.
- SmCo is clearly current and flagship, but the page introduction is not SmCo-specific.
- Each prior project exposes research focus, methods, contribution, and status/type.
- Shining 3D is not presented as an academic research project.
- Desktop and mobile layouts have no horizontal overflow.

## Approved Image-led Card Revision

- Replace the four text-only Research Areas cells with a wide reuse of the approved conceptual workflow image, followed by a compact four-area index.
- Render all research work through one reusable project-card model with optional authentic media.
- SmCo uses the featured card variant spanning the full two-column grid. It keeps two concise verified outcomes and the Workflow Console CTA; detailed parameters remain in Console.
- Dual-laser PBF, battery FE-ANN, thermal-shock fracture, and SiC use standard image-led cards in a two-column desktop grid and one-column mobile layout.
- Shining 3D remains an Industry R&D card and may render without an image.
- When no verified result image exists, remove the media region entirely. Never show a fake result, generated scientific evidence, or empty image placeholder.
- Each retained image requires an honest caption and local provenance in the project data.
